Ket Gala | Culture-First Viral Campaign

Ket Gala

The play: A hyper-local, red-carpet spoof of the global Met Gala, decoded through Bengaluru's street-smart humour, irreverent styling and witty commentary instead of a straight copy of the trend.

The effect: A culture-first content property that made a global format instantly shareable in Kannada, positioning Radio City as a brand that reimagines trends rather than just following them.

Take It Easy Policy | Civic Partnership Campaign

Take It Easy Policy

The play: Built with Bengaluru City Police, the campaign borrowed the language of policy and governance to deliver one simple ask: breathe, slow down, and be patient on the city's roads.

The effect: Institutional credibility met Radio City's signature humour, turning a preachy public-safety message into a conversational, youth-friendly one.

Civic issue Cultural insight Humour Police partnership Public participation Behavioural change

Vote Maadi | Voter Awareness Campaign

Vote Nalli Maja Ide

The play: A series of short-form reels that swapped the conventional "Go Vote" announcement for humour, everyday situations and Radio City's local voice, meeting young audiences exactly where they already are.

The effect: Turned a serious civic mandate into content that felt native to the feed instead of delivered at it, using entertainment as the on-ramp to real participation.

Public mandate Local insight Humour & relatability Social storytelling Youth engagement Civic action

Waterless Holi | Sustainability-Led Festive Campaign

Holi: Waterless

The play: With Bengaluru's water crisis front of mind, the campaign used Holi itself, not a lecture about it, as the platform for behavioural change, urging citizens to celebrate without wasting water.

The effect: Made sustainability feel participative and positive rather than restrictive, pairing festive energy with environmental responsibility.

Artificial Independence | Independence Day Campaign

Artificial Independence

The play: A provocative Independence Day idea: we won freedom from external forces, but how dependent have we become on technology? The Chief Minister of Karnataka took a tongue-in-cheek "Artificial Independence Oath" on air, giving it scale and credibility.

The effect: A chatbot-powered journey let audiences take the oath themselves and share it organically, amplified by an original track, RJ-led programming and celebrity participation into a full entertainment moment.

Solar-Powered Radio | Sustainability Innovation

Sunkranti

The play: India's first solar-powered radio show: Radio City ran an entire broadcast on solar energy alone, turning the medium itself into the message instead of just talking about sustainability.

The effect: A tangible proof-of-concept, amplified across a multi-part digital series that took audiences behind the innovation and built an early leadership voice in green broadcasting.
